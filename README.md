What to Consider Before Buying a Luxury Flat?

Before you buy luxury flats in Gurgaon, consider the property's location, developer reputation, construction quality, amenities, floor plan, connectivity, and long-term maintenance requirements. Location is particularly important because different areas of Gurgaon offer different residential environments. Factors such as access to workplaces, schools, hospitals, shopping centres, public transport, and major roads can influence both daily convenience and future demand.
